Turn on alternate sources?

Some episodes and movies are hosted on third-party video players instead of being streamed directly.

  • More to watch. Unlocks episodes and movies that are only available from those players.
  • Higher quality. Adds extra sources to the player's Sources menu.
  • Pop-up ads. These players may open ads or new tabs. An ad blocker, such as uBlock Origin, stops most of them.

If you leave them off, you'll only see videos streamed directly, with no ads. You can change this any time in Settings.
